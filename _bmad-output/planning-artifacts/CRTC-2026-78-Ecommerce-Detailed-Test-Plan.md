## 1. Purpose, scope and interpretation

This plan turns the strategy into individual executable test cases for a typical provider's ecommerce storefront and self-service experience: Internet, wireless, home phone, TV, service plans and promotions, smartphones, watches, tablets, and security/IoT. Cases cover compliance, ecommerce functionality, regression, uncommon situations, failures, accessibility, and operational evidence. Actual system names and enabled features are unknown and must be mapped before execution.

**Regulatory boundaries:** CRTC 2026-78 requires an easy-to-use self-service mechanism for applicable telecommunications plans, informed and express consent, immediate effect to the greatest extent possible, written confirmation of self-service actions with the expected confirmation timeframe stated in the mechanism, and no customer-service access fees. The policy sets 26 April 2027 for implementation/enforcement of the self-service requirement. It does not impose a universal confirmation deadline, a specific website or app, or a numerical usability or processing SLA. Existing accessibility and privacy protections apply. The plan's technical and commercial checks, evidence practices, performance targets and release thresholds are provider controls, not additional mandates from this policy.

Legal/regulatory must decide applicability for TV-only offerings, hardware, financing, IoT, prepaid, specific add-ons and complex business contracts. Do not assume a product is covered merely because it appears in ecommerce. Other rules and contracts apply; in particular, an action fee is not automatically permissible simply because it is not a customer-service access fee. The policy references Telecom Regulatory Policy 2026-43 regarding activation and modification fees. An enterprise-wide review of customer-service access fees across non-ecommerce channels is outside this plan.

## 2. Execution instructions and common prerequisites

### 2.1 Before starting

1. Regulatory/legal and product approve the strategy's A1–A6 decisions: the product/action applicability and eligibility inventory; designated self-service mechanism(s); account roles; disclosure, consent and written-confirmation rules; fee rules under 2026-78, 2026-43 and other applicable requirements; and the effective-time/exception rules. Record versions and approvals. An undecided applicability or fee rule is **Blocked**, not Pass.
2. Obtain approved expected-result sources independent of the UI under test: contract and catalogue versions, plan entitlements, fee/tax/credit matrices, promotion and finance schedules, role permissions, billing and provisioning rules, approved translated content, supported platforms, accessibility standard and operational recovery objectives. Record source versions with each run.
3. Use a representative non-production environment with safe provision/billing endpoints or verified integration environments, controlled clock and fault injection. Obtain read-only access to customer, consent, order, provisioning, inventory, payment, billing and message-delivery records. Use sandbox payment instruments and monitored test inboxes; never modify a real customer's service.
4. Prepare the following **synthetic fixtures** with resettable balances and independent expected outcomes. Substitute actual product identifiers and values in the execution record; do not assume they exist in production.

| Fixture | Required state and independently documented oracle |
|---|---|
| **I** | Individual account holder with active Internet, two eligible higher/lower plans, approved recurring prices/terms and reachable test inbox. |
| **W** | Individual with two active wireless lines, eligible higher/lower plans and add-on; one smartphone with an outstanding finance balance; one linked watch with a separate service relationship. Record actual dependencies and approved balances. |
| **H** | Individual with active home-phone service and eligible plan/feature changes. |
| **S** | Small-business account with multiple lines, account holder, an authorized user with a defined subset of permissions and a user without those permissions. |
| **B** | Internet + TV + home-phone bundle and qualifying wireless discount; record prices, credits, equipment and consequences if Internet is removed. |
| **L** | Grandfathered plan with an expiring promotion and approved non-restorable prior terms; also prepare a current plan with restorable terms. |
| **T** | Standalone TV package with equipment and optional channels; legal classification recorded. |
| **D** | Compatible smartphone, watch and tablet variants, accessories, SIM/eSIM, available stock and a distinct out-of-stock item. Record compatibility and finance schedule. |
| **X** | Security/IoT product with service/installation dependencies and an incompatible option. Record whether underlying connectivity is a telecommunications plan. |
| **P** | Promotions: valid, expired, ineligible and non-stackable; independently calculated expected current/future prices, credits and dates. |
| **A** | Multiple accounts and customer roles; valid/invalid credentials; monitored and invalid/bouncing confirmation destinations; accessible alternative if approved. |
| **Q** | Pending, suspended, overdue and disputed accounts with documented legal/contractual expected eligibility; sandbox outcomes for approved/declined/pending credit and payments. |
| **E** | Controlled clock, taxes for at least two supported jurisdictions, billing boundary, fault-injection controls, notification inbox and known transaction IDs. |

5. Where a case says **“complete the action”**, use the designated mechanism, review the disclosure, actively provide consent and submit without a live representative. Where it says **“reconcile”**, compare the customer selection and consent with the order status, actual service/entitlement effective state, applicable billing/invoice entries and received written message. Capture request, effective and delivery timestamps. A pending order or queued message alone is not evidence of completion or delivery.
6. Execute parameterized cases **once for every applicable row** in the approved product-family × action × individual/small-business inventory and each designated mechanism. Run authorized-role variants and current/legacy, bundle and high-risk variants as specified. Assign each execution a unique suffix, e.g., `SS-01-I-UP-WEB`, and create a separate result record. Reset fixtures between runs. Mark a case N/A only with a documented inventory reference and approval.
7. For cases involving delayed notifications, billing cycles or asynchronous fulfilment, record an interim state but leave the case **Not run / pending** until the actual final outcome and received message are checked. Fault-injection cases require explicit authorization and must not run against production.

### 2.2 Recording results and priority

In every grid, the first column gives the case ID, priority, rule and originating strategy identifiers; the next columns are the requested tester fields. For each instantiated execution, record exact data/variant, date, tester, build, browser/device, timestamps, observed values and links to secured evidence in **Actual result**. Enter **Pass**, **Fail**, **Blocked**, **N/A** or **Not run** in the final column. **Blocked/N/A/Not run are not passes**; include owner and reason. A failure must have a defect ID and impact.

**P0** = critical regulatory or high-impact control; **P1** = important functional/integration control; **P2** = lower-frequency coverage. Severity of an actual defect is separately assessed. Execute positive and negative outcomes as independent cases. Where a source specifies no exact threshold, use a previously approved provider criterion and retain its version; do not invent a regulatory deadline.

## 11. Entry, exit and deliverables

**Entry:** A1–A6 have owners and approved decisions for the selected scope; independent expected results and legal fee rules are available; fixtures and controlled integrations are validated; accounts and destinations are safe and resettable; evidence access and defect workflow are ready. If a dependency is unavailable, affected tests are blocked and the gap is reported.

**Proposed release exit:** 100% of applicable product-family × action × individual/small-business rows executed on the chosen mechanism; 100% of applicable P0 executions passed; all R1–R7 mapped to approved tests and evidence; no open critical/high defects affecting compliance, unauthorized changes, material billing, privacy or a critical accessible journey; required written confirmations received and reconciled; non-immediate effects investigated and justified; prohibited access fees absent in tested order/bill records. Applicable regression packs must pass, or remaining non-P0 exposure must be documented and approved. These are QA targets, not numerical requirements stated by the CRTC. A waiver does not establish legal compliance.

**Test completion report:** provide coverage by product/action/segment/role/mechanism, case and iteration results, blocked/N/A reasons and owners, defect list and retests, legal interpretations, accessibility/privacy/security outcomes, request-to-effective and notification evidence, billing reconciliations, residual risks, and named QA, product, operations and regulatory/legal decisions. Retain records according to approved privacy and retention policy.
